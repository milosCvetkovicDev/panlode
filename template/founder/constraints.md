## Vetoes

Defaults that keep a solo, bootstrapped founder away from long roads to the first euro. Delete the ones
that do not apply to you and add your own.

- Hardware.
- Regulated licensing (a licence is needed before the first sale).
- Two-sided marketplaces.
- Consumer products that depend on virality or paid acquisition.
- Enterprise sales cycles (months of procurement, demos and security reviews before the first euro).
- Products whose running cost breaks the tooling budget (self-hosted models, GPU inference, large data
  warehouses).
- <Any restriction from an employment or client contract on side projects, competing work or IP.>
